Battery Testing and Data Analysis

Li-Polymer Battery Cycle-Life Testing

VLRCOE, Georgia Tech · Undergraduate Researcher / Test Operator · Jan–May 2025

Performed battery-pack cycling using a published charge/discharge protocol, collected pre/post-discharge internal-resistance measurements, and maintained test records. Reviewed capacity data and subsequently analyzed internal-resistance results in Excel.

Program context: The eight-pack longitudinal study had accumulated roughly 4,700 combined cycles at the interim-report stage.

Published protocolResistance measurementExcel analysis
Protocol and apparatus

The study used 6S Li-polymer packs, charging at no more than 1C, cooling to ambient temperature, resistance measurements before and after discharge, and 750 W constant-power discharge. The apparatus used West Mountain Radio battery-analysis equipment and LabJack T7-Pro logging.
